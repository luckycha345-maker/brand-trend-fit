## Acceptance probes

1. Replay supported direct tasks and unseen paraphrases across multiple industries. Require capability quotes and defensible needs, not just shared words.
2. Try the same terms against other product profiles. Shared holiday, audience or the word home must not decide relevance.
3. Make an unrelated term rank one, an exact seed and a frequent prior choice. It must remain withheld in every ranking mode.
4. Remove/negate the relevant capability or select a different function. Reassess rather than reusing stale preference decisions.
5. Duplicate a term across a regional total and multiple filtered charts. Retain each original ordinal/filter set; no minimum-rank substitution across charts.
6. Supply search-result order, stale evidence or a conflicting region. Do not admit them to verified-ranked recommendations.
7. Separate constructed probes from real-source replay, disclose label corrections, and report small or non-independent samples honestly.
